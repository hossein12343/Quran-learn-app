Data we process

We process your email address, account identifier, display name, learning progress, bookmarks, and settings needed to provide the service. If you enable notifications, a browser push subscription is processed to deliver reminders.

Why we process it

We use this data to authenticate you, synchronise learning progress, operate reminders, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability. We do not sell personal data.

Service providers and retention

Account and synchronisation data are hosted using Supabase. The website is delivered through Cloudflare. Data is retained while your account is active, subject to reasonable operational, security, and legal retention needs.

Your choices

You can disable notifications in the app or browser. Contact the publisher to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Add the publisher’s legal name, contact address, jurisdiction, and retention periods before publishing.
